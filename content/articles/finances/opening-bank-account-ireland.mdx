You will need an Irish bank account for salary, rent, and everyday spending. The process is straightforward once you have the right documents.

## What banks usually ask for

<ChecklistItem id="bank-1">Passport and IRP card (or visa proof)</ChecklistItem>
<ChecklistItem id="bank-2">Proof of Irish address (utility bill or lease)</ChecklistItem>
<ChecklistItem id="bank-3">PPS number (some banks allow application before PPS arrives)</ChecklistItem>

## Online vs branch

Revolut and N26 are popular for day-one spending, but many employers prefer paying into a traditional Irish current account. AIB, Bank of Ireland, and PTSB are common choices.
