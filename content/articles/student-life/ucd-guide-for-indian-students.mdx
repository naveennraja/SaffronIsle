University College Dublin (UCD) is one of the most popular choices for Indian students coming to Ireland. Here is what helps in your first semester.

## Indian societies and community

UCD has active Indian student groups that run festivals, study sessions, and peer support. Join their social channels in Welcome Week — it is the fastest way to find housemates and friends.

## Part-time work on Stamp 2

<Callout type="tip">
Stamp 2 students can work up to 20 hours per week during term and 40 hours during designated holiday periods — check the exact dates each year.
</Callout>

## Budget and housing

Belfield campus is well connected by bus and Luas. Many Indian students start in on-campus accommodation, then move to shared houses in Stillorgan, Rathmines, or Sandyford.

<ChecklistItem id="ucd-1">Register with the international office</ChecklistItem>
<ChecklistItem id="ucd-2">Open a Irish bank account after getting address proof</ChecklistItem>
<ChecklistItem id="ucd-3">Join the Indian society WhatsApp groups</ChecklistItem>
