A PPS number is your personal public service number in Ireland. You need it for work, tax, and most official business.

## Who needs a PPS number?

If you are working, opening a bank account, or registering for tax in Ireland, you almost certainly need one. Students on Stamp 2 often need it for part-time work too.

<Callout type="tip">
Bring your passport, proof of address, and reason for applying (employment letter or college letter) to your appointment.
</Callout>

## Step-by-step process

<ChecklistItem id="pps-1">Check if you already have a PPS on revenue.ie</ChecklistItem>
<ChecklistItem id="pps-2">Book or walk into your local Intreo office</ChecklistItem>
<ChecklistItem id="pps-3">Submit documents and wait for your number by post</ChecklistItem>

## Dublin vs other cities

Dublin Intreo offices are busiest. If you are in Cork or Galway, local offices often have shorter queues — but the documents required are the same nationwide.

<Callout type="warning">
Processing times can vary. Do not wait until your first payday to apply.
</Callout>
