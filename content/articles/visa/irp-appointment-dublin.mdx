The Irish Residence Permit (IRP) is your registration card in Ireland. For most Indian students and workers, booking the IRP appointment is one of the first admin tasks after arrival.

## When to book

<Callout type="info">
This section applies to Stamp 2 holders who must register within 90 days of arrival.
</Callout>

Book as soon as you have your local address and required documents ready. Slots fill quickly in September and January.

## What to bring

<ChecklistItem id="irp-1">Book IRP appointment online at irishimmigration.ie</ChecklistItem>
<ChecklistItem id="irp-2">Bring passport, visa, proof of address, and college or employment letter</ChecklistItem>
<ChecklistItem id="irp-3">Pay the registration fee (card accepted)</ChecklistItem>

<Callout type="warning">
This rule changed in January 2025 — always check INIS for the latest fee and document list.
</Callout>

## After your appointment

You will receive your IRP card by post. Keep photocopies of everything you submitted — employers and banks may ask for them.
