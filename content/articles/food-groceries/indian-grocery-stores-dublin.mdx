Missing home food is real. Dublin has plenty of Indian and South Asian shops once you know where to look.

## Northside and city centre

Areas like Parnell Street and Moore Street have several shops selling dals, atta, pickles, and frozen parathas. Prices are often better than mainstream supermarkets for bulk rice and spices.

## Southside favourites

Stillorgan, Dundrum, and Tallaght each have stores catering to the Indian community. Weekend mornings are busiest — go early for fresh vegetables.

<Callout type="tip">
Lidl and Aldi are brilliant for basics — pair them with a monthly trip to an Indian grocer for spices and specialty items.
</Callout>

## Halal and specialty items

Ask locally in WhatsApp groups for updated recommendations — shops open and close, but the community always knows the current best spots.
