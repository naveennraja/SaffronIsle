I still remember the flight from Bangalore — excited, terrified, and carrying one suitcase too many of instant noodles.

## The first month

Dublin felt cold and quiet compared to home. Finding a room took three weeks of viewings. WhatsApp groups from other Indians were what saved me — someone always knew a landlord, a grocer, or a doctor.

## What I wish I knew earlier

<Callout type="tip">
Start your IRP and PPS processes in your first two weeks. Everything else — job, bank, lease — gets easier after that.
</Callout>

## One year on

I have a favourite chipper, a cycling route along the canal, and friends who feel like family. Ireland is home now — not instead of Bangalore, but alongside it.

If you are mid-move: it gets better. Really.
